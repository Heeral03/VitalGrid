02

O(h) Hierarchy & Heartbeats

Locks are bound to spatial tree depth ($O(h)$). Active staff apps or IoT monitors send lightweight 10s telemetry heartbeats to renew 30s leases.

03

Auto-Release & Zero Drift

If heartbeats stop, the system lock naturally expires in 30 seconds, automatically surfacing available bed capacity across the network.
